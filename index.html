Our scans

Ultrasound services

Every scan comes with a written report the same day, and time to ask questions. Preparation notes are included below so you arrive ready.

OBS · 1st–3rd trimester

Obstetric scans

Pregnancy dating, growth and wellbeing checks, anomaly screening and presentation scans — with clear explanations at every stage of your pregnancy.

How to prepareEarly pregnancy: drink 3–4 glasses of water 1 hour before and don't empty your bladder. Later scans: no preparation needed.
ABD · Upper abdomen

Abdominal scans

Liver, gallbladder, pancreas, spleen and kidneys — for abdominal pain, suspected gallstones, and general check-ups requested by your doctor.

How to prepareDo not eat for 6–8 hours before your scan. Water is fine. Morning appointments are easiest — just skip breakfast.
PEL · Pelvis

Pelvic scans

Uterus, ovaries, prostate and bladder assessments — for irregular periods, pelvic pain, fibroid checks and fertility work-ups.

How to prepareArrive with a full bladder: drink 4–5 glasses of water 1 hour before and hold it until after the scan.
SMP · Small parts

Thyroid, breast & scrotal

High-frequency scans of the thyroid, breast lumps, scrotum and other superficial structures, with careful documentation of any findings.

How to prepareNo preparation needed. Wear clothing that allows easy access to the area being scanned.
MSK · Soft tissue

Musculoskeletal & soft tissue

Shoulders, tendons, muscle injuries, swellings and lumps under the skin — useful for sports injuries and unexplained soft-tissue masses.

How to prepareNo preparation needed. If you have previous scan reports or X-rays, bring them along.

Your visit

What to expect

Most visits take 30–45 minutes from arrival to report in hand.

STEP 01

Book or walk in

Call or WhatsApp us to book, or simply walk in during opening hours. Bring your doctor's request form if you have one.

STEP 02

Your scan

The scan itself is painless and typically takes 15–25 minutes. We explain what we're looking at as we go.

STEP 03

Report & next steps

You receive a printed report the same day, with images where relevant, ready to take back to your referring doctor.
